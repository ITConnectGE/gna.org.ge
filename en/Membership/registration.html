Joining the Association

Membership in the Neuropsychology Association offers members a wide range of professional development opportunities that will help deepen specialists' competence and systematically develop the field in Georgia.

Membership benefits:
  • Priority access to planned seminars, webinars, and trainings
  • Opportunity to attend and participate in specialized certified courses
  • Access to specially developed resources, materials, and modern methodological approaches
  • Involvement in research projects and conferences
  • Opportunity to share professional experience with colleagues
  • Opportunity to receive consultation and mentorship from experienced specialists
  • Participation in the development of professional standards and recommendations
  • Participation in increasing the accessibility of neuropsychological services
  • Opportunity to publish articles on the Association's platforms
Who can become a member?
Membership in the Association is open to:
  • Holders of a master's degree in neuropsychology
  • Psychologists
  • Specialists interested in field-related research
  • Students who want to deepen their professional knowledge
  • Anyone interested in the field
Membership categories

Membership in the Neuropsychology Association is available in the following categories:

Full Member
Individuals holding the relevant academic degree (e.g., master's or doctoral degree in psychology, neuropsychology, or a related field) with at least 2 years of professional experience.

Associate Member
Specialists with relevant education and/or experience in psychology, education, medicine, or other related fields who are interested in the development of neuropsychology. Associate members have the opportunity to be involved in professional and educational processes, but do not have voting rights in the Association's strategic planning.

Student
Bachelor's or master's-level students studying psychology or related disciplines, interested in the theoretical and practical aspects of neuropsychology.

To apply for membership, please send us your CV and motivation letter. We will contact you after reviewing your application. Contact
